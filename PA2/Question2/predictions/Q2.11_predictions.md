# Q2.11 — Predictions before training models B–D

Written and committed **after** "Testing the Baseline" (Tables 3–4, Figures 3–5) and **before** any of models B, C, D
(or D+B) was trained or any model-D candidate was compared.

Failures found in Testing the Baseline: model A is only mildly inconsistent under mirror and rotation (rotation worse),
and it breaks down on speed already at 2× and completely at 3×.

**Prediction (three sentences):** Model B (symmetry augmentation) will shrink C_M and C_R and bring E_M and E_R down to
its own E(5), fixing the appearance-tied part of the mirror/rotation failure, but it will not help at 2× or 3×. Model C
(speed augmentation, same architecture) will clearly improve 2× because about 6.6 px/frame is still within the 8 px reach,
but it will stay poor at 3× (about 10 px/frame) because that motion exceeds the network's one-step reach. Model D (larger
reach, speed augmentation) will be the only model that also works at 3×, with E(2) at 3× much closer to its 1× value,
while none of B–D should lose more than a little accuracy on the original 1× clips.
